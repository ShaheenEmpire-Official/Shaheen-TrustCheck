🌍 Shaheen TrustCheck

Global AI-Powered Trust, Scam Detection & Digital Security Platform

Check Before You Click, Buy, Download or Trust.

Shaheen TrustCheck is a global digital trust and security platform designed to help people identify suspicious websites, online stores, mobile applications, job offers, messages, phone numbers, and potential online scams.

Our mission is to make digital trust and online safety accessible to everyone, everywhere.

🎯 Our Mission

Build a powerful, reliable, multilingual and evidence-based global platform that helps users make safer decisions online.

🚀 Core Features

- Website Safety & Trust Checker
- Online Shopping Scam Detection
- Mobile Application Risk Analysis
- Job Offer & Recruitment Verification
- Suspicious Message & Phishing Analysis
- Phone Number Risk Intelligence
- Social Media Account Risk Analysis
- Global Scam Reporting System
- AI-Powered Risk Analysis
- Multilingual Global Support
- Evidence-Based Security Reports
